Timeline from the lecture: viruses and worms (1980s to 2000), ransomware (2010s, attackers encrypt data and demand payment), advanced persistent threats (long-term, hidden espionage by organized or state groups, can use any malware form), and now AI-powered attacks (generative AI, automated malware) plus adversarial AI (attacks against AI systems themselves). The course covers using AI to attack, using AI to defend, and attacking AI.

Ransomware is its own kind of malware, not a virus or worm. New areas such as IoT and cloud security bring their own issues.

For SmartCity Accra: IoT devices and cloud services are on the list of areas the lecture said need defending.
