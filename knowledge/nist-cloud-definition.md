Cloud computing is a model for enabling ubiquitous, convenient, on-demand network access to a shared pool of configurable computing resources. The definition comes from the US National Institute of Standards and Technology (publication SP 800-145). Key words the lecturer stressed: "ubiquitous" (reachable from anywhere with a connection) and "configurable" (you are not locked in; if a choice is too costly or does not fit, you reconfigure or move).

It is the base for the five characteristics and the service and deployment models.
