Final lab (3 hours plus 2-3 hour extension), covering all three Prosits. Honesty over polish.

Parts and deliverables:
- Part A (60 min): one advanced feature, scoped small: digital-twin what-if replay, multi-cloud fallback, or federated-learning demo. Deliverable A: 300-word test report.
- Part B (45 min): one-page integration design linking two Prosits (AgriHealth, Urban-Rural, or your own): data flow, event/API, one data-sovereignty or consent issue. Deliverable B.
- Part C (60 min): consolidated final architecture doc (AgroConnect Wk 5, HealthConnect Wk 9, SmartCity Wks 10-13, integration design, lessons-learned page). Deliverable C.
- Part D (30 min): timed 10-15 minute rehearsal with feedback from another team. Part E (10 min): decommissioning plan with owner and date.

Final submission (via Canvas, due before Week 15, 12-19 Dec revision/exam week): feature report, architecture document, code and docs repo link, slides, recorded demo video link.

Rubric (final project = 20% of course grade): advanced feature 15%; integration design 15%; architecture document 20%; presentation clear to technical and non-technical audiences 25%; code quality and documentation 20%; decommissioning plan 5%.
