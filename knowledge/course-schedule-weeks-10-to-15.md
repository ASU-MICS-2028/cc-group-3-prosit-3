As planned in the syllabus (dates may shift; handouts win over syllabus lines):
- **Week 10:** scalability and performance, auto-scaling, monitoring (Erl ch. 12, CloudWatch docs); lab on monitoring and optimisation; homework a 500-word performance plan.
- **Week 11:** cost management (Erl ch. 13, Cost Explorer docs); lab analyse and cut costs; 500-word cost plan.
- **Week 12:** edge computing and IoT (reading "Edge Computing: Vision and Challenges"); lab an edge/IoT component; 1,000-word paper review.
- **Week 13:** machine learning in the cloud and MLOps (SageMaker docs; "MLOps: Continuous Delivery"); lab train/deploy a model; 500-word ML justification.
- **Week 14:** multi-cloud and sustainability (reading "Multi-Cloud Computing"); final demo; 1,500-word research proposal; 10 to 15 minute presentation.
- **Week 15:** revision and exams.

Prosit 3 (SmartCity Accra) runs Weeks 10 to 14. Lectures Monday 1:15 to 2:45 pm, lab Thursday 8:00 to 11:15 am.

**Also (Course Description Fall 2026 (schedule); Lab 10-14 docs):** Week dates: 10 = 10-14 Nov, 11 = 17-21 Nov, 12 = 24-28 Nov, 13 = 1-5 Dec, 14 = 8-11 Dec, 15 = 12-19 Dec (revision and exam). The 300-word report for lab N is due before the Week N+1 Monday lecture.
- Wk 10: performance optimization report (lab + 500-word proposal due Wk 11); Wk 11: cost optimization (500-word plan due Wk 12); Wk 12: edge/IoT integration (1,000-word review of the edge paper due Wk 13); Wk 13: ML component (500-word justification due Wk 14); Wk 14: advanced feature test (300 words) plus code and docs, presentation (10-15 min) and a 1,500-word cloud-trend proposal due Wk 15.
Lab reports in Labs 10-14 describe their own contents; see each lab note.
