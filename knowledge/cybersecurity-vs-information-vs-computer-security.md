---
title: Cybersecurity, information security and computer security differ by what is protected
tags: [security, lecture]
source: Lecture 3
---

All three share the same principles; they differ in scope:

- Information security is the broadest: any information, including paper and other non-digital forms.
- Computer security covers a computer, even a standalone one not connected to the internet.
- Cybersecurity focuses on cyberspace (connected systems and the internet) and defending its use from cyber attacks.

Once systems are connected to the internet the lines blur and all the principles apply. The course mostly uses the term cybersecurity.
