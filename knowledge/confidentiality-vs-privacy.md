---
title: Confidentiality is non-disclosure of any data; privacy is an individual's control over their own data
tags: [security, privacy, lecture]
source: Lecture 3
---

Confidentiality means information is not disclosed to unauthorized individuals, entities or processes. It applies to any data, including organizational data, and is achieved with technical and organizational protection such as encryption.

Privacy is the right of individuals to control how their personal information is collected, used and stored: you decide what to share and may stay anonymous. Privacy and anonymity are related but not the same (people may know who you are yet not know certain things about you).

So privacy is tied to confidentiality but is narrower: personal data and personal control. Confidentiality is about secrecy of whatever must not be disclosed.

For SmartCity Accra: air, waste and water sensor data is mostly not personal, so confidentiality (who may read it) matters more than privacy; any data tied to residents (for example reported locations) would raise privacy questions.
