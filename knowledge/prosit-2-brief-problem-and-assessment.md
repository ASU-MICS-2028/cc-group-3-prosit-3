Problem in numbers: 1 doctor per 10,000 people (WHO suggests 1 per 1,000); 40% of Ghanaians live more than 5 km from a health facility; specialists concentrate in Accra and Kumasi; 83% own a mobile phone; 15,000+ trained CHVs.

Four themes: (1) community health records (offline PWA, fingerprint login, five languages, Act 843, FHIR, OAuth/JWT, conflict resolution, GHS integration); (2) telemedicine (video on 2G/3G, appointments, e-prescriptions, mobile-money fees, image sharing, referrals, WebRTC, fraud checks, escalation); (3) disease surveillance (dashboard, maps, ML prediction, alerts, DHIMS2 and WHO); (4) sustainable ecosystem (insurance, traditional medicine, supply chain, training, revenue, SDG 3).

Targets: 99.7% uptime, zero breaches, telemedicine on 2G, 50,000+ records, 1,000+ concurrent users. Assessment in the brief: technical 40%, community impact 25%, innovation 20%, autonomous leadership 15%.

How the team read it: far bigger than four weeks, so build the foundation properly and document the rest honestly.

SmartCity: expect the same shape (numbers, themed weeks, targets, a long wish-list to scope).
