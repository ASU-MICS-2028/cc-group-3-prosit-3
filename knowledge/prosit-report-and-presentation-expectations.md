At the Prosit 1 closing session the instructor said each prosit is assessed in two parts: the presentation and the written report. Technical proficiency, presentation skill, teamwork and the level of detail all count.

Expectations stated in class:
- Own everything you present. Follow-up questions are expected, and "the AI wrote that part" is not an acceptable answer.
- The report is documentation, written like software documentation, not a book: under about fifteen pages.
- Follow the report format the instructor sends, section by section, instead of submitting a free-form write-up.
- Do not let a strong presentation be followed by a thin report; the report should be as detailed as the work done.
- Presentations are scored by the other student groups as well as the instructor, and the instructor tallies them. Groups do not score themselves.
- The instructor also said the documentation is meant to be a personal artifact: it lets you learn the parts of the system you did not build yourself.

For SmartCity Accra: plan the report outline early and have every member able to defend every section.
