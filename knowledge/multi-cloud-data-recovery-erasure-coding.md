Multi-cloud means combining several independent providers rather than one. In the reviewed paper, data is encrypted and split with erasure coding into chunks spread across providers, so lost chunks can be rebuilt from the rest. It reported 79 percent shorter recovery time and about 4 times throughput across seven Amazon locations.

Reviewer concerns: small test scale, complexity growing in enterprise networks, and whether particle swarm optimisation counts as reinforcement learning.

For SmartCity Accra: a reference for backup and disaster-recovery discussion beyond a single region.
