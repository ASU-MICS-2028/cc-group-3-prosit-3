Each brief week follows CESI CLARIFY / EXPLORE / SYSTEMATIZE / IMPLEMENT.
- Week 1, Urban Intelligence Foundation: multi-sensor IoT (LoRaWAN/NB-IoT), edge computing, ingestion of thousands of readings per minute, offline resilience, device authentication and encryption, citizen monitoring app. Choices: IoT platform, communication tech, edge hardware, pipeline (Kafka/Kinesis/Event Hubs), time-series DB (InfluxDB/TimescaleDB).
- Week 2, Intelligent Urban Analytics: forecasting (air quality 24-48 h ahead, waste fill, energy, traffic), anomaly detection, orchestration (Airflow/Kubeflow/Azure ML), MLflow/DVC, low-latency serving, A/B testing, monitoring and retraining.
- Week 3, Autonomous City Optimization: traffic light AI, waste routing, multi-agent coordination, reinforcement learning, digital twin, explainable AI, federated learning, chatbots.
- Week 4, Democratic Smart City Ecosystem: AMA dashboard, citizen app, open data portal, community participation, revenue model, community ownership, SDG 11 dashboard.
The 4 brief weeks are separate from the course weeks 10-14, whose labs map onto them.
