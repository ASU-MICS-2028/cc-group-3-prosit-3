title: Public, private, hybrid and community cloud
tags: [cloud-basics, lecture]
source: Lecture 2
---

- Public cloud: shared infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP); lowest cost and fastest to start.
- Private cloud: dedicated infrastructure, used for very sensitive government or bank data.
- Hybrid cloud: a mix, e.g. a public-cloud app plus government-owned on-premises data. The lecture called this the likely reality for agriculture.
- Community cloud: shared by several organisations with common concerns, e.g. a regional African health cloud linking centres of excellence (used for genome sequencing during COVID).

Lecture 1 added the practical angle: a startup begins on public cloud, scales elastically, and keeps especially sensitive data on its own server so customers see it takes security seriously.
