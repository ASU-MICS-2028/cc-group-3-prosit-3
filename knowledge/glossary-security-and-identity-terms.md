IAM manages identities and access. MFA needs several verification factors. Encryption at rest protects stored data; in transit (TLS) protects moving data. Zero trust verifies every user and device. RBAC grants access by role, ABAC by attributes. PKI manages keys and certificates; HSM and KMS/CloudHSM protect keys. For SmartCity: per-device identity and encrypted transport for sensors.
