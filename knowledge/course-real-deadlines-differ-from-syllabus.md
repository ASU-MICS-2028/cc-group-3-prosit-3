In Prosit 2 Lab 5 was due 22 Oct at 23:59 and Lab 6 on 25 Oct, not on the syllabus dates; the Week 9 handout and final format arrived late. The team's rule: take deadlines from Canvas, the repo README or the facilitator, never from the syllabus table, and confirm Labs and the final format early.

It is also unclear whether the weekly 500-word homework reports in the syllabus are required (the lab handouts do not mention them); the team raised it with the facilitator. Reflection questions count toward participation. Homework extensions are optional but support the innovation and autonomy marks.

For Prosit 3: confirm the Lab 10 to Lab 14 due dates and the final presentation format in week 10.
