After Prosit 2 the lecturer said the next posting is about how to scale: distributed processing paradigms across wide geographies with millions of concurrent users, not just cloud deployment. A system that struggled at 1,000 concurrent users must be understood and redesigned for far more. Read the MapReduce paper first.
