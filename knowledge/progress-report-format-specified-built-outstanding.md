---
title: A progress report states what was specified, what is built, and what is outstanding
tags: [process, course]
source: Lecture 10
---

Instructor feedback on progress presentations: do not just talk. Say (1) what the specification required, (2) what you have built, (3) what is left, so the audience can judge a percentage complete. Name owners per remaining task. Teams reported about 80% done with remaining items such as reminders, payments and end-to-end language support.

Also: every team member must be able to explain the whole system, and the report is what gets graded, so it must pull everything together in the template.
