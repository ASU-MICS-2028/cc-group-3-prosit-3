tags: [security, lecture]
source: Lecture 3
---

Beyond confidentiality, integrity and availability, security professionals added two goals: authenticity (genuine origin, non-repudiation) and accountability (actions traceable to users). Together they form the pentad of five elements. Loss of any of these properties for data or services is considered a security breach.

Digital assets are anything valuable (data, services, information). Losing confidentiality, integrity or availability, being impersonated, or being unable to trace what happened are all security issues. The lecture said these five goals are interlinked and that the course builds on them.
