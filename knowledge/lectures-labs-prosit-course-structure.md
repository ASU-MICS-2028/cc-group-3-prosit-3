Lecture 1 explained a change made after last year's feedback. Handing students a whole project and hoping they would learn on the side led to last-minute rushes. Now:

- Roughly 50 slides of lectures give just-in-time knowledge.
- Weekly three-hour lab sessions (Thursdays) break the project into small pieces. Each lab has visuals to follow, sample code and a homework extension.
- Labs are submitted on schedule. When a prosit is launched, the team mostly assembles what the labs already produced; the lecturer said that should take about a week or two.
- Lab reports include short reflection questions (about 300 words).
- A "week zero" set of prerequisites (Linux, Docker, GitHub, databases) is ungraded but expected; see [week-zero-prerequisites](week-zero-prerequisites.md).
- Teams of three to four, each with a name and assigned roles (front end, back end, data, community liaison). Team work is meant to mirror industry.

For SmartCity Accra: treat the labs as the building blocks of the final system rather than separate exercises.
