Rehearse the full timed run with feedback from another team on pacing, whether a non-technical listener can follow the first 3 minutes, and demo reliability. Record a narrated backup demo (under 5 minutes) of the most reliable feature, and check it plays on another machine. Running over time is a common deduction. Proofread the architecture document so diagrams match what is actually running.
