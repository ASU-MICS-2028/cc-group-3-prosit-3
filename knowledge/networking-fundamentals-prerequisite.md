During the load balancer review the instructor noticed students struggling with TCP handshake and layer concepts. He said the course assumes the OSI model and the TCP/IP layers, and promised a short reading (5 to 10 pages) on them.

Minimum to recall: layer 4 balancing works on TCP connections and addresses; layer 7 sees HTTP. The SYN, SYN-ACK exchange starts a TCP connection.
