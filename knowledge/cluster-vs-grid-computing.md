---
title: A cluster is tightly coupled and uniform; a grid is heterogeneous and geographically dispersed
tags: [cloud-basics, course]
source: Study Session 1
---

Cluster: independent IT resources interconnected to work as one system. Grid: computing resources organised into logical pools coordinated as a high-performance distributed grid, heterogeneous and geographically dispersed. Redundancy and failover with redundant high-speed links support availability.
