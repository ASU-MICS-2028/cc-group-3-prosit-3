Weights: quizzes and tests 15%; lab assignments and projects 25%; mid-semester exam 15%; attendance and participation 5% (lab reflection questions count here, they are not graded themselves); final exam 20% plus final project 20% (40% together). Each prosit is also judged on technical excellence, community impact, innovation and autonomous learning; Prosit 2's launch deck used a 400-point scale (technical 40%, innovation thinking 25%, Ubuntu leadership 20%, autonomous learning 15%) and expected daily learning logs and weekly peer evaluations.

Late work: at most 85% after 1 day, 75% after 2 days, 60% after 3 days, then 0 unless a documented reason was given before the deadline.

Practical effect: a lab due at 23:59 is worth rushing; the Prosit 2 team submitted Lab 5 in a two-hour sprint.

**Also (Lecture 1):** The grade mixes several parts. Weights as stated in Lecture 1 (the lecturer said some details could still change):

- Mid-semester assessment: 15%.
- Project component: 25%.
- Class attendance and participation: 5%.
- Quizzes: taken after each major topic is finished, 30 to 45 minutes of lab time. They mix calculations with multiple choice / true-false.
- Content-dependent tests ("testlets"): a short industrial scenario followed by several sub-questions on it. Usually two per topic, one framed on AWS and one on Google Cloud or Azure, often with a calculation.
- Final exam: described as about 20%, but if all the projects are covered through the semester that 20% is folded into the project side instead.

Why it matters for SmartCity Accra: Prosit 3 is the last of the three projects, so it carries the heaviest "apply it to a scenario" load. Expect calculations (cost, capacity, latency) and cross-provider reasoning, not only AWS recall.

**Also (Course Description Fall 2026 (Class Policy)):** Assignments, projects and lab reports are penalized when late: maximum score 85% after one day, 75% after two, 60% after three, 0% after that unless a documented compelling reason is given before the deadline. A missed assessment scores zero without a provable reason notified beforehand. Since lab reports are due before Monday lectures, finish by the weekend.
