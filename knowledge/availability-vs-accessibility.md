---
title: Availability is about the system being up; accessibility is a different idea
tags: [security, reliability, lecture]
source: Lecture 3
---

Availability means authorized end users can use information systems and data whenever needed. A service outage, deleted data, a hacked system that locks you out, or a forgotten or changed password all count as availability problems.

Accessibility is a different concept (ability of a person to use something, for example with disabilities, or the separate topic of access control). The lecture insisted the word to use in security is availability, not accessibility.

Availability is more than "completely down": delay and degraded quality of service also count (see the related note). Typical protections are backups, redundancy and failover servers.

Example: a hospital's electronic health record system must be available to doctors around the clock.

For SmartCity Accra: flood and drain alerts are only useful if the platform stays available during a storm.
