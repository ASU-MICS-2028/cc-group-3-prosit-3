Lab 13 says a model that loses to the naive baseline is a valid, gradeable finding if reported honestly (common causes: too little data, signal close to random). Lab 14 repeats this for advanced-feature failures: reasoning quality is graded, not whether the feature worked. Backfilled synthetic data is acceptable if stated.
