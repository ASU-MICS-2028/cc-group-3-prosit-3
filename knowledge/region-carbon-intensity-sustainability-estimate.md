Run the architecture through AWS Customer Carbon Footprint Tool, Microsoft Sustainability Calculator or Google Cloud Carbon Footprint. If the tool needs a paid tier you do not have, documenting which region you would choose for its lower-carbon grid and why is an accepted substitute. The report should name one trade-off, e.g. cheaper region versus cleaner-grid region, or serverless versus always-on for one component.
