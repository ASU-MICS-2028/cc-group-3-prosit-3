The brief lists traffic, air quality (PM2.5, CO2, NO2), waste, water, energy and flood sensing. The team scoped to three: air quality (PM2.5), waste bin fill level and flood/drain water level. Traffic, water flow and energy sensing, computer vision, and the larger AI features (multi-agent, RL) are not built and should be stated as out of scope. Lab 12 allows one domain fully built (extension: three simultaneously). Flood is the city-safety domain the lab's reflection asks about.
