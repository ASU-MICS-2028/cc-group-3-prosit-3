---
title: Essay answers are marked on the itemized points, not word count
tags: [course, lecture]
source: Lecture 8
---

For essay or calculation questions with a suggested length (e.g. 350 words), you get credit for answering each itemized component of the marking scheme; you do not need to hit the word count. Answer on point and fast.
