Lecture 1 gave a data-centre visit anecdote: operators use more than one network service provider so that if one goes down, service continues. The lesson: do not depend on a single vendor, and understand the service level agreement (SLA) you sign. This links to the outcome on multi-cloud orchestration.

For SmartCity Accra: note single points of failure (one region, one broker) and what the SLA actually promises.
