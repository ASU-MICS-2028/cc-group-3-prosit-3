---
title: Stop idle cloud resources to protect credits
tags: [cost, cloud-basics]
source: Lecture 1; Lecture 2
---

Anything you start keeps running and billing until you stop it. Both lectures warned that forgotten VMs and containers eat the free credits, leaving a surprise bill. The lecturer planned account setup to include a safeguard from the first lab. A student in the lab session also described setting a monthly budget (about 5 USD) with a forecast alert so you are warned before crossing it.

For SmartCity Accra: tear down load-test and ML-training resources right after use, and set a budget alert on day one.
