tags: [course]
source: Lecture 6; Lecture 5

- Question types: true or false with justification, calculations, and explain-the-concept.
- Quiz answers must touch every expected point; writing generically loses marks (for example listing 3 of 5 items gives 6/10).
- Mid-semester was moved from week 7 to about week 10 or 11, run as a full Thursday lab slot to simulate exam conditions, with heavier calculations.
- Final exam is 3 hours (3-credit course).
- Grading is generous per the lecturer, but exams are hard.
- The third project (SmartCity) is part of the final exam.
