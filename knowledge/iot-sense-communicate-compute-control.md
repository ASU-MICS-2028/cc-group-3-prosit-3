title: IoT loop: sense at the edge, communicate over MQTT to a central store, compute and control
tags: [iot, mqtt, lecture]
source: Lecture 10; Lecture 11
---

The lecturer defined IoT and edge computing as three steps: measure with edge sensors, communicate the data (via MQTT) to a central repository or cloud database, then compute a decision and send control back to the edge device ("go up or down, do something with the vehicle").

Prosit 3 (smart city) is described as building on Prosit 1 and 2 pieces, with the sensors as the new element on top: how to measure, how to send from the edge to a cloud-hosted database, and how to compute and send control back. It is not a rebuild of AgroConnect or HealthConnect.
