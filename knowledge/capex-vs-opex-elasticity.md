---
title: Capex versus opex - why elastic cloud capacity beats owned servers
tags: [cloud-basics, cost, scaling]
source: Lecture 2
---

Owning servers is capital expenditure: a fixed capacity bought up front that does not change when demand does, plus the cost of staff to maintain it. Renting from the cloud is operating expenditure: capacity follows demand. When customers arrive you add containers or instances; when sales drop you remove them and pay less.

Lecture 2 used the December-peak shop and the 1990s client/server era (organisations built and ran their own infrastructure, found it expensive to maintain, and moved to subscription) as the story.

For SmartCity Accra: the cost analysis should compare a fixed-size deployment with an auto-scaled one under the same sensor load.
