Teaching team: Prof. Robert A. Sowah (instructor), Dr. Stephane Nwolley (co-instructor) and Shullamite Konama Darteh (faculty intern, the facilitator). Lectures Monday 1:15 to 2:45 pm; lab Thursday 8:00 to 11:15 am; Patrick Nutor Hall 115.

Method: problem-based learning (the CESI model). Teams of up to four solve a real problem (a prosit) over roughly four weeks; lectures and labs give just-in-time knowledge. Each prosit ends with deliverables graded on technical, community impact, innovation and autonomous learning.

Learning outcomes in one line: choose IaaS/PaaS/SaaS; design scalable, fault-tolerant, cost-aware systems; automate with CI/CD; secure data; monitor and tune cost and performance; use edge/fog for latency-sensitive work; judge trends (AI ops, green cloud, multi-cloud); present as a team to technical and non-technical audiences.

Core books: Erl, Mahmood and Puttini; Wilder, Cloud Architecture Patterns; Hightower, Burns and Beda, Kubernetes: Up and Running; also NIST SP 800-145 and Kleppmann ch. 5.
