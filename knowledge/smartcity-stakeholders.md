Primary users per the brief: city officials (AMA dashboards, planners, service managers, emergency coordinators); urban citizens (2.5 million residents, commuters, business owners); community organizations (neighbourhood associations, civil society, traditional leaders, youth groups); technology ecosystem (local developers, startups, researchers, international partners). Community integration model also names religious organizations, women's groups and the informal sector. Practical implication: an AMA dashboard plus a citizen app, with transparency and inclusion of informal settlements.
