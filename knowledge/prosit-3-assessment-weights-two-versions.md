Two versions exist, so ask the staff which applies.
- Brief: Technical Competence 40%, Community Engagement (Ubuntu in urban context) 25%, Innovation Leadership 20%, Autonomous Learning 15%.
- Launch deck rubric preview (slide 53): technical competence 40%, urban community engagement 30%, AI learning process 20%, smart city collaboration 10%. Another launch slide lists 40/25/20/15 over 400 points with different category names.
The technical 40% and the 20% line agree; the community and learning weights differ. The lab docs say optional extensions feed the Innovation and Autonomous Learning dimensions.
