AgroConnect was framed with real farmer groups, and the rules are the model for later prosits:

- Name, phone number, GPS coordinates and financial details are sensitive; collect them only with prior consent and only what the system needs.
- Involve community leaders and follow local decision-making protocols.
- No production financial transaction without instructor sign-off.
- Trials need authorisation from the programme or coordinator.
- Academic honesty: AI tools must not generate submitted work unless explicitly allowed for that task.
- Dummy data is fine for early development.

For SmartCity Accra: if citizens' reports or locations are collected, treat them the same way; sensor readings from public devices are lower risk.
