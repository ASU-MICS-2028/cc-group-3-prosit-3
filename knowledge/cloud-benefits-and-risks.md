---
title: Cloud benefits (responsiveness, lower upfront cost, availability) and risks (trust boundaries, lock-in, cost overruns)
tags: [cloud-basics, cost, security]
source: Study Session 1
---

Benefits: increased responsiveness, reduced investment with proportional cost, increased availability and reliability.
Risks: overlapping trust boundaries and shared security responsibility, more cyber exposure, less operational governance, limited portability between providers, multi-region compliance and legal issues, cost overruns.
For SmartCity Accra, cost overruns and compliance are the live ones: set budgets and alarms.
