Teams may use AWS, Azure or GCP (accounts on free tiers or student credits, roughly 100 to 200 USD of credit mentioned). Every choice must be justified technically; "everyone uses AWS" is not an answer, in the same way service-model choices must be argued. Lecture 2 set the first task as opening accounts on all three free tiers. Class chat noted the AWS UI and free-plan terms change often, so check current conditions and use the school email where required.
