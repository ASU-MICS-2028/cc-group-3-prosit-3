The course uses a problem-based model (borrowed from a French technical-education tradition meant to train people already in industry to solve their own problems). A real, unsolved problem is given in week one and a working system is expected by week fourteen. The cycle repeats once per prosit (three times a semester):

- Clarify: what exactly is being asked? (AgroConnect: a platform linking farmers and buyers, with delivery, payment and a way to verify farmers.)
- What do we not yet understand? Name the knowledge gaps (databases, networking...) and read up; a teammate strong in an area fills in the rest.
- Explore: what real users and stakeholders need, and what literature or similar systems (e.g. in Kenya, Rwanda) show.
- Systemize: design the architecture, pick tools, state the trade-offs.
- Implement: build, test, reuse, and measure real impact.

For SmartCity Accra: write the problem statement and gap list first; the report should show the trade-off reasoning, not just the final build.
