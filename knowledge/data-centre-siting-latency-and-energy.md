Lecture 1 framed a real tension. Nobody wants a data centre next door (noise and heat at full load), but pushing them far away adds latency from distance and equipment delays, which hurts latency-sensitive applications. That is why edge and fog computing exist. Providers also optimise energy by shutting down idle servers and migrating workloads across regions as demand shifts by time of day; AI-enabled cloud management (Google's work was cited) and green computing are course themes, and the problem is formulated mathematically.

For SmartCity Accra: Accra sensors talking to a distant region is a latency and energy trade-off worth quantifying; edge filtering reduces both.
