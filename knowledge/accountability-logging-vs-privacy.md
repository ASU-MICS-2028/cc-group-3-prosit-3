---
title: Logging for accountability trades off against privacy, and policy sets the balance
tags: [security, privacy, lecture]
source: Lecture 3
---

A student raised that logging every action is a privacy concern. The lecture's answer: yes, but you cannot be fully private inside an organization's systems. By accepting an organization's terms and policies (for example network monitoring) you accept some loss of privacy; that is organizational policy for protecting the systems, not a privacy violation.

A separate case is telemetry and error reports, which software collects to maintain and debug systems; this serves reliability and need not be about tracing individuals.

For SmartCity Accra: log actions on the platform, but avoid collecting more personal data than needed.
