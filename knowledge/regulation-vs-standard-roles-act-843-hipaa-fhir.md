Our summary: Ghana's Data Protection Act 843 says whether and why data may be collected (justify each field, explicit consent); HIPAA gives methods for safeguarding; FHIR R4 gives the data shape (UUID ids, standard observation codes). Consent was checked per action, not once. Hosting in af-south-1 keeps data in-region since Ghana has no hyperscale region.
