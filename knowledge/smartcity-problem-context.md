AMA and the Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development partner with the university to build an integrated AI/ML/IoT platform that optimizes urban services and supports sustainable development, appropriate for African infrastructure. Partners named: AMA, Ghana Smart Cities Initiative, Accra Digital Centre, University of Ghana Smart City Lab, East Legon Community (pilot neighbourhood), Makola Market Association.

Urban problems cited: traffic (3+ hours daily commuting), waste (60% improperly managed), energy loss (40%), irregular water (70% of urban areas), flooding from poor drainage, rising air pollution, informal settlements (40% of urban dwellers). The brief's opportunity: sensors for real-time awareness, ML forecasting, AI optimization, citizen participation, data-driven policy. Launch-deck figures are course-supplied claims; treat as context, not verified facts.
