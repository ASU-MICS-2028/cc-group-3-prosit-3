---
title: Pre-record a screen capture with voiceover so a live failure cannot derail the demo
tags: [process, course]
source: Lecture 10 (instructor guidance)
---

The instructor recommended: while testing, when everything works, record the screen with a voiceover and play it during the presentation. Live demos fail (one team's mobile money demo failed on network that day). Keep a live link as a bonus, not the only plan.
