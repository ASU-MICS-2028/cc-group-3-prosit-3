---
title: Architecture review with four fixed questions
tags: [process, course]
source: Lab 8 Part B
---

Reviewers ask in order: 1) where does this break under 10x load; 2) where is the single weakest security link; 3) what happens if the network drops mid-consultation or the DB is briefly unreachable; 4) one open question specific to the design. Presenters answer live, then privately revise the diagram. Keep a before and after: no "before" is not evidence of revision. Note disagreements instead of dropping them.

Useful as a self-check before the SmartCity design review.
