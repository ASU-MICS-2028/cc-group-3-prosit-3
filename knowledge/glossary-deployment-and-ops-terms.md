Canary deployment releases to a small subset first (Lab 13's 10% model canary uses the idea). Blue-green swaps traffic between two identical environments. IaC defines infrastructure in files; GitOps runs it from Git. CI/CD automates integration and release. MLOps joins data science and operations. A/B testing compares two versions. Chaos engineering injects failure to build confidence (like Lab 12's buffering test). SRE applies software engineering to operations.
