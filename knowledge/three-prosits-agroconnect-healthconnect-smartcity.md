The semester runs three community-oriented projects in order:

1. AgroConnect Ghana: an agricultural platform where buyers order fresh produce directly from farmers, see the farm location and a route, and pay online. Aim: remove middlemen so farmers keep more of the price.
2. HealthConnect Ghana: a rural telemedicine platform, motivated by the shortage of doctors and nurses in rural areas.
3. SmartCity: the final project (this repo's Prosit 3, SmartCity Accra).

Lecture 1 noted that in an earlier year the class could not even reach SmartCity because the load was too high, which is why labs now pre-build the pieces. Each prosit follows the same problem-based cycle once.
