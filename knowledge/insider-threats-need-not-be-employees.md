title: Insiders are anyone related to the organization, not only employees
tags: [security, lecture]
source: Lecture 3
---

The lecture's warm-up listed everyday issues that count as security problems: copyright infringement, unauthorized use of someone else's card, identity theft, eavesdropping, data loss, denial of an action, and insider threats. An insider can be a student, contractor or anyone related to the organization with some motive (curiosity, proving cleverness, money). Motives were promised for a later lecture.

Takeaway: security is not only about big external attackers; ordinary actions with digital assets fall under it.
