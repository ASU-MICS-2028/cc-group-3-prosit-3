title: Prosit 3 expects daily learning logs and weekly peer evaluations
tags: [prosit-3-brief, process, course]
source: Prosit 3 launch deck slides 22, 41, 44; Course Description

Launch deck: daily learning logs (about 25 minutes, with reflection on urban intelligence), weekly peer evaluations using Ubuntu principles, expert and official feedback, a progressive portfolio. Time expectation: 40 team hours a week, about 10 hours per member (roughly 2 hours per day, weekdays). The course description also requires weekly peer evaluations and weekly reflective reports (formative 60%), teams of at most four with documented roles and contributions, and peer-support credits.
