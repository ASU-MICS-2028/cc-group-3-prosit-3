---
title: The CIA triad (confidentiality, integrity, availability) is the core of security
tags: [security, lecture]
source: Lecture 3
---

The three primary goals of security are confidentiality (no unauthorized disclosure), integrity (data and systems stay accurate and are changed only by authorized people or processes) and availability (authorized users can use the system whenever they need it). The lecture used the NIST definition of cybersecurity, which frames it as protecting and restoring information and communication systems so these three properties hold.

If any one of the three is compromised, that is a security breach. A useful habit from the lecture: when you meet any incident, ask which of the three goals it broke and what it led to next.

For SmartCity Accra: sensor readings must not leak (confidentiality), must not be tampered with on the way to the broker (integrity), and the dashboards and alerts must stay up during a flood (availability).
