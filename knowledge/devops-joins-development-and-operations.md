title: DevOps joins software development and IT operations
tags: [ci-cd, process, lecture]
source: Lecture 4
---

Traditionally developers want more resources while the IT operations side manages shared infrastructure, so the two clash. DevOps merges both responsibilities in one practice: building software and also running and maintaining the infrastructure. It goes beyond development alone. A cloud engineer needs solid OS, networking and software engineering foundations.
