Rules

Sand is a fill with dark text. Never sand text on a light screen.

Never colour alone. A status is always an icon and a word.

Glass is a translucent card over the screen glow. It falls back to solid on phones that cannot blur.

Every text and background pair is checked: python3 design/check_contrast.py

7 · Shape and space

Radius: --radius 28px cards · --radius-sm 16px inputs · --radius-pill buttons and nav · circles for dials, bubbles and handles.

Space: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 px (--space-1…6). Touch targets are at least 44 px (--tap).

Screens: .screen with data-theme="light" or "dark". Dark screens carry a warm glow behind the hero, the way the reference's charcoal screen does.
